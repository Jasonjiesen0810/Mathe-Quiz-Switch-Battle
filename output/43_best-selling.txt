=== SPRECHERTEXT ===
The most talented person in the room is often the one who earns the least. Robert Kiyosaki once met a journalist in Singapore. Her writing was excellent. Her novels went nowhere. He told her to take a course in selling. She was offended. She had a master's degree in English literature. So he pointed at her own notes. She had written: best-selling author. Not best-writing author. Talent alone is rarely enough. You also need the skills that carry it to people. His advice was simple. Work to learn, not just to earn.
