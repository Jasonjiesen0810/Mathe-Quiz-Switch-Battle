=== SPRECHERTEXT ===
Discipline is choosing between what you want now, and what you want most. Right now, you want comfort. Your bed. Your phone. One more episode. And that voice is loud. It never gets tired. It always has a reason. But there's another voice. It doesn't shout. It whispers. It's the voice of the person you could become. Every night, both voices ask you for the same hour. And every night, you choose who gets it. So turn the loud one down. Close the app. Open the notebook. Do one more hour. Not because it feels good. Because you want it most.
