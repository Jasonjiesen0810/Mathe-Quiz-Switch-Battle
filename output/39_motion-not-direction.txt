=== SPRECHERTEXT ===
Being busy is the most comfortable way to stay exactly where you are. It feels like progress. Emails answered. Meetings attended. A to-do list full of checkmarks. You go to bed exhausted, so it must have been a good day. But look at what actually moved. Nothing important did. Busy is motion. Progress is direction. Brian Tracy calls it the vital few: a handful of tasks create most of your results. So tomorrow, before you answer anything, do the one task that actually moves you. Less motion. More direction.
