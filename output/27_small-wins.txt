=== SPRECHERTEXT ===
Small wins are not small. You make your bed. You finish one task. You send that one email you've been avoiding. It doesn't look like much. But something changes inside your head. Brian Tracy says it in Eat That Frog: once you begin a valuable task, you're naturally motivated to continue. Your brain loves the feeling of done. And it wants that feeling again. One win gives you energy for the next. Then the next. Then the next. That's momentum. So don't start your day with the hardest mountain. Start with one small win, and let it pull you forward. Small wins build big days. And big days build a different life.
