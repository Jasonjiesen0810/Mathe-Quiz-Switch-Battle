=== SPRECHERTEXT ===
The people who fail the most often win the most. Sounds backwards, right? We're taught that failure is the opposite of success. So we avoid it. We wait until we're sure. We only try what we know we can win. And we count every failure like a scar. But look closer. Every person you admire has a longer list of failures than you. Because they weren't counting failures. They were counting attempts. Each try taught them something the person who waited will never learn. So stop protecting your record. Raise your number of attempts. Fail more. Fail faster. That's how winning starts.
