=== SPRECHERTEXT ===
The house you're proud of might be the reason you're not getting rich. That's one of the most controversial ideas in Rich Dad Poor Dad. Robert Kiyosaki's rich dad used a simple test. An asset puts money in your pocket. A liability takes money out. Now look at the house. The mortgage. The repairs. The taxes. Every month, money leaves. So by that test, it's a liability. Rich dad put it like this: the rich acquire assets. Everyone else acquires liabilities, and thinks they're assets. It's not how much money you make. It's how much you keep. So before you buy anything, ask one question. Which way does the money flow?
