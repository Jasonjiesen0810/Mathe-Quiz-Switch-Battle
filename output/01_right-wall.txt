=== SPRECHERTEXT (ganze Sätze, Pausen nur am Satzende) ===
You are always busy, always moving, always climbing. But being busy is not the same as making progress.
Stephen Covey said it well: if your ladder leans against the wrong wall, every step only takes you further to the wrong place.
Brian Tracy puts it simply: clarity comes first.
Remember this: never perfect a task that should not exist at all.
Step one: be specific. Vague goals bring vague results. Not: get fit, but: run 5K by June.
Step two: every morning, ask yourself: does this move me closer to my goal? If not, it can wait.
Find the right wall. Then climb.
