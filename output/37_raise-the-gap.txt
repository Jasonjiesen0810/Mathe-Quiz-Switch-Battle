=== SPRECHERTEXT ===
Getting a raise is one of the fastest ways to stay broke. And almost nobody notices it happening. Here's how it works. You earn three thousand a month, and you spend all of it. Then you get a raise. Four thousand. A nicer apartment. A better car. Dinner out, because you deserve it. A year later, you're spending four thousand. You earn more than ever, and you're exactly as broke as before. Because wealth was never about what you earn. It's the gap between what you earn and what you spend. So the next time you get a raise, don't raise your life. Raise the gap.
