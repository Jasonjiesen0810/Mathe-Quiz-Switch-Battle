=== SPRECHERTEXT ===
The most disciplined people you know have the least willpower. And that's exactly why they win. Most of us think discipline means fighting temptation all day. Phone on the desk. Snacks in the kitchen. Notifications on. Every hour, a new fight. And every fight costs you a little more. Disciplined people don't win those fights. They remove them. Phone in another room. No snacks in the house. Notifications off. Nothing to resist, so nothing to lose. Stop training your willpower. Design your environment.
