Stop telling everyone your plans. It feels good to say it out loud. People nod. They're impressed. You feel like you already did something. And that's the problem. Your brain gets the reward before you do the work. So the plan stays a plan. Do it the other way around. Work quietly. Let them hear about it when it's done. Plans talk. Results speak.
