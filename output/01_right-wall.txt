=== SPRECHERTEXT (Mischung: merkbare Zitate + klare Sprache) ===
You're always busy. Always moving. Always climbing… But busy is not progress.
Stephen Covey put it perfectly: If the ladder leans on the wrong wall… every step gets you to the wrong place. Faster.
Brian Tracy calls clarity perhaps the most important concept in productivity. Clarity comes first.
Remember this: Never perfect a task that shouldn't exist.
Step one: be specific. Vague goals get vague results. Not: get fit. Run five K by June.
Step two: every morning, ask: Does this move me closer? If not, it can wait.
Find the right wall. Then climb.
