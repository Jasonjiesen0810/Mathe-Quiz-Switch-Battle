At nine years old, Robert Kiyosaki worked for ten cents an hour. And it became the most valuable job he ever had. Every Saturday, he worked in a store owned by the man he called his rich dad. After three weeks, he was angry. He wanted a raise. Rich dad didn't give him one. He took the pay away completely. Because the low pay was the lesson. The poor and the middle class work for money. The rich have money work for them. Most people spend their lives asking for a raise. Very few ask how to build something that pays them, even when they're not there. Which question are you asking?
