=== SPRECHERTEXT (einfaches Englisch, direkte Ansprache) ===
You work hard. Every single day. You climb and climb… But where are you going?
Stephen Covey said: If your ladder is on the wrong wall… every step takes you to the wrong place. Faster.
Brian Tracy says: clarity comes first. You need to know what you want.
Here is the problem: You can do a task perfectly… and it can still be useless.
So, step one: Decide what you want. Not: get fit. Say: I will run five kilometers.
Step two: every morning, ask: does this help my goal? If not, do it later.
Find the right wall. Then climb.
