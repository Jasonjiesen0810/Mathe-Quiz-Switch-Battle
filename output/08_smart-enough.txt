=== SPRECHERTEXT (af_bella) ===
Being smart can make you stupid. Here is what I mean. Smart people see every risk. They read one more book and make one more plan. They know exactly why every idea could fail. So they wait.
And while they are still thinking, someone with half their knowledge is already doing it. Because knowledge without action is just an expensive way to stay stuck. Overthinking feels like progress. But it is not. So here is the rule: if you have researched it for weeks, you are not learning. You are hiding. Take one imperfect step today.
Smart people plan. Successful people start. Be smart enough to start.
